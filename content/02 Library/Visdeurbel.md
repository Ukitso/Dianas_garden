Fish doorbell 

"Every spring, thousands of fish swim through Utrecht’s canals on their way to the Kromme Rijn to spawn. Along the way, they reach the Weerdsluis, a historic lock that is still operated by hand. When the lock gates are closed, the fish have to wait. This uses up valuable energy and makes them easy prey for birds and predatory fish.

To help them on their journey, an underwater camera has been installed at the lock. If you spot fish on the livestream, press the doorbell. The lock keeper will receive a notification and, when enough fish are waiting, will open the lock. That way, you help the fish continue their migration safely."
