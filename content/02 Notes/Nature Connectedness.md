# Nature Connectedness

I'm interested in how people relate to nature, not only how often they're outside. This page connects research on that relationship with the everyday experiences and conditions that shape it.

Nature connectedness can involve what people notice, understand, value and feel about the environments around them. The notes here follow those ideas through wellbeing, childhood, outdoor time, education, access, language and culture.

## Nature and connection

- [[Nature Connection]] explores how people notice and engage with natural features.
- [[Nature, Language & Culture]] maps research on language, vocabulary and cultural perspectives.

## Everyday experience

- [[Time Outdoors]] collects findings about frequency, duration and nature exposure.
- [[Outdoor Play]] looks at children's opportunities for unstructured outdoor play.
- [[Waldkindergarten]] follows research on forest kindergartens and nature-based early education.

## Wellbeing and access

- [[Nature & Wellbeing]] gathers research on mental health, emotion and cognition.
- [[Access to Nature]] explores who can reach and use green spaces, and what gets in the way.

Some of these questions also connect with [[HELIO®]].
