# Nature, Language & Culture

I've been interested in how language and culture shape the way we understand and relate to the natural world.

The words we use, the metaphors we associate with nature and the cultural experiences we grow up with can all influence how we see our surroundings. I'm particularly interested in how different ways of describing nature might shape whether we see ourselves as separate from it or as part of it.
