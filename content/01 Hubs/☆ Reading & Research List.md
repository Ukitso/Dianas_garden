# Reading List & Cultural References

Things I’ve come across and want to explore. This is a discovery page, not full reading notes.

## Theory of the Stretched Image

**Essay by August Kaasa Sundgaard**, part of *This Can’t Be the Place*.

I came across this through a TikTok about stretched images online. I’m curious about how these distortions become part of internet culture, and how platforms shape the images we make and see.
![[this can't.png|276]]
**Explore:** image distortion, social media formats, how visual trends spread.

**Links:** [Original TikTok](https://www.tiktok.com/@dazy_chains/video/7699185477169073428) · [*This Can’t Be the Place*](https://attachments.are.na/46279706/848137c4a3343cd75fefb5e9436c67ed.pdf)

## Exit Reality

**Book by Valentina Tanni (2024).** [*Exit Reality*](https://www.neroeditions.com/product/exit-reality-2/)

It looks at internet-born aesthetics and digital subcultures. I’m interested in how online spaces mix nostalgia, surrealism and the uncanny, and start to feel like their own worlds.

**Explore:** vaporwave, weirdcore, liminal spaces, the Backrooms, digital nostalgia.

## Carrier Bag Fiction

**Publication (2021).** [*Carrier Bag Fiction*](https://mediathek.hkw.de/en/publication/carrier-bag-fiction-en)

I want to explore its connection to Ursula K. Le Guin’s idea of storytelling as gathering, rather than only focusing on a hero or a weapon.

**Explore:** [Le Guin’s “The Carrier Bag Theory of Fiction”](https://www.ursulakleguin.com/the-carrier-bag-theory-of-fiction), storytelling, and the connection to “man the gatherer.”
