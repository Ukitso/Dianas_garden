# Nature, Language & Culture

I've been interested in how language and culture shape the way we understand and relate to the natural world.

The words we use, the metaphors we associate with nature and the cultural experiences we grow up with can all influence how we see our surroundings. I'm particularly interested in whether the way we describe nature shapes how we see our place within it: as something separate from us, or something we're part of.

## Exploring the subject

### Language & perception

- [[Nature & Language]]
    
    Linguistic relativity, disappearing nature vocabulary and the metaphors we use to describe the natural world. Exploring how language might influence perception, attention and our relationship with nature.
    

### Culture & media

- [[Nature in Culture & Media]]
    
    Nature in books, music, film and contemporary media. Looking at how cultural representations reflect our relationship with the natural world, and how they might shape it in return.
