[[Bauhaus]] [[Creativity]]

A **Renaissance man** is someone who is skilled across lots of different areas, not just one. The idea comes from the Renaissance period, where people believed humans should develop **a wide range of abilities**.



### Core idea

**Be well-rounded, not specialised.**

### What that looks like

Instead of being _just_ a designer, or _just_ a scientist, you explore multiple fields and let them inform each other.



- art + science + philosophy + craft
- thinking creatively **and** logically
- learning through curiosity, not just necessity



### Famous example

- Leonardo da Vinci → artist, engineer, scientist, inventor



### Why it matters

- different skills = **new perspectives**
- ideas cross over → more original thinking
- problem solving becomes more flexible



### In one line

> A Renaissance man is someone who builds creativity by connecting different disciplines, not staying in one lane.
