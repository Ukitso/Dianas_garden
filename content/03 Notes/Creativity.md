[[Aphantasia]] [[Bauhaus]] [[Renaissance man]]
I don’t create visually  
so I create through everything else

and that approach reflects a broader truth

**creativity was never meant to be one thing**

We’ve moved away from being 'well-rounded creators'  
and towards narrow, visual-first creativity

But:
**true creativity comes from working across disciplines, materials, and inputs**

And:
**not being able to “see it” might actually bring you closer to that**

**Creativity isn’t a single skill.**

**_It’s a system._**
