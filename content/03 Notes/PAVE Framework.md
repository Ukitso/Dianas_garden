# PAVE Framework

PAVE is a social-first creative framework built around four stages: Purpose, Audience, Value and Execution.

It looks at social as a behaviour rather than just a collection of platforms, and culture rather than a content calendar. The idea is to think beyond what content to make and consider why people would care about it, what they get from it and what might make them want to share or take part.

## P: Purpose

- What is the idea for beyond selling something?
    
- What problem, obsession or belief is it tapping into?
    
- How does it earn its place in culture?
    

Purpose is about finding the reason behind an idea. What is it trying to say or change? Is there something people can connect with beyond the product or service itself?

## A: Audience

- Which platforms and communities are already part of the audience's life?
    
- What language and formats feel native there?
    
- What prompts interaction: sharing, flexing, nostalgia, humour, drama or a shared interest?
    

This is about understanding how people actually behave online, rather than making assumptions based on platform demographics. Where do they spend their time? What do they engage with? What conversations, communities and cultural references are already part of their world?

## V: Value

The framework identifies four types of value an idea can offer:

- **Entertainment:** Memes, humour, chaos or drama.
    
- **Utility:** Useful shortcuts, hacks, tools or practical ideas.
    
- **Identity:** A sense of belonging, shared references or an aesthetic people recognise as their own.
    
- **Emotion:** Validation, pride, empathy or inspiration.
    

An idea doesn't necessarily need to offer all four. The question is what someone gets out of engaging with it. Is it useful, entertaining, relatable, inspiring, or something they can use to express themselves?

## E: Execution

- What does the idea look like when it appears naturally on social?
    
- Which formats suit it: short videos, stitches or duets, carousels, hooks, longer captions, AI remix culture, drops, comment threads, point-of-view posts or live content?
    
- Is there a clear point of view, and is the idea designed to be shared?
    

Execution is where the thinking becomes something people can actually interact with. Rather than choosing a format first and trying to fit an idea into it, the format should follow from the purpose, audience and value.

## A note to myself

The useful thing about PAVE is that it gives you a way to think through an idea before jumping straight into content formats or platforms. It brings the focus back to the people on the other side of the screen.

Why would someone care? What would they get out of it? And what would make them want to participate?
