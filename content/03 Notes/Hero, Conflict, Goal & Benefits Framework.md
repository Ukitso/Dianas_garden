# Hero, Conflict, Goal & Benefits Framework

A framework for mapping out the people at the centre of a project, the problem they're facing, and the change the project wants to create.

![[Pasted image 20261123142545.png|393]]

The framework has six parts:

## Hero

Who is at the centre of the project?

- Who are we trying to help?
    
- Who is affected by the problem?
    
- Who needs to be part of the change?
    

The hero is the person or community the project is ultimately about.

## Means

What is the intended outcome of the project?

This is where the desired result or overall ambition is established. What do we want to make possible?

## Values

What does the hero believe in or care about?

- What matters to them?
    
- What do they want to protect or achieve?
    
- What shared beliefs or principles bring them together?
    

Values help establish what the project is trying to preserve or build on.

## Conflict

What is getting in the way?

- What is the problem?
    
- Why does it exist?
    
- What is stopping the hero from achieving what they want?
    
- What needs to change?
    

The conflict gives the project something to respond to. It creates the tension between the current situation and the desired outcome.

## Goal

What are we trying to achieve?

The goal should respond directly to the conflict. It describes the change the project wants to make happen.

## Benefits

What does success look like for the hero?

- What improves if the goal is achieved?
    
- Who benefits from the change?
    
- What becomes possible afterwards?
    

This is the wider positive outcome of the project.

## How the framework connects

The Hero, Means and Values help establish who the project is for and what matters to them. The Conflict identifies what's standing in the way. The Goal responds to that conflict, and the Benefits describe what the change could mean for the people involved.
