# Familiar Strangers

People we repeatedly encounter or recognise without ever actually interacting with them.

Think of someone you see every morning on your commute, the person who visits the same coffee shop, or someone you regularly pass on the street.

## Key ideas

- **Familiarity without interaction:** We recognise people through repeated encounters, even without knowing their names or anything about their lives.
    
- **Invisible relationships:** These people become part of our everyday routines and surroundings without becoming friends or acquaintances.
    
- **Mutual recognition:** You might recognise someone, but rarely consider that they might recognise you too.
    
- **Sociometric stars: People who are particularly recognisable within a shared social environment, even if they're not personally known.**

	  *"...like that one person you see at the airport who stands out, 
	  and you know when you see them at the baggage claim that you're 
	  in the right place"*


- **Changing contexts:** Encountering a familiar stranger somewhere unexpected can break the invisible boundary and turn recognition into interaction.

## An interesting thought

**We're all somebody else's familiar stranger.**

Someone might recognise us from their daily commute, remember what we wear, or notice when we're absent, without us ever knowing.

We tend to think of ourselves as the main character in our own everyday lives, but we're also recurring characters in countless other people's lives.
