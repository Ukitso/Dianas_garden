'DESIGN IS DEAD'

Italian designer known for being quite anti-consumerist and very principled about design.

**Core ideas**

- **Design should be honest** → no unnecessary decoration, no fake materials
- **Anti-consumerism** → against overproduction and throwaway culture
- **Democratising design** → people should be able to make things themselves
- **Process > product** → understanding how something is made matters more than just owning it

**Autoprogettazione (key project)**

- A series of furniture designs made from **standardised wooden planks + nails**
- Anyone could build them using basic tools
- The goal wasn’t just the chair, it was **learning through making**

NOT [[Bauhaus]] but related in mindset

- Bauhaus → design for industry and mass production
- Mari → often pushes _against_ mass production, encouraging people to **make things themselves**

reminds me of [[Accessibility in design]] strong connection as its designed human first, with tha human condition in mind
